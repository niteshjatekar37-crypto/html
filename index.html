Our story · Rooted in Rajasthan

A land of colour, craft and timeless stories.

Rajasthan is known for its golden Thar Desert, grand forts and palaces, colourful bazaars, folk music and vibrant festivals. From the pink streets of Jaipur to the blue lanes of Jodhpur and the lakes of Udaipur, every region has its own character and creative traditions.

We celebrate the people behind these traditions: artisans who shape, paint, weave and decorate each piece by hand. Small differences in colour, pattern and finish are part of the charm of handmade work—each item has its own story.

Handicrafts with a story

Traditional skills passed from one generation to the next, made with patience and imagination.

Folk art

Kathputli Puppets

Rajasthani kathputli puppets are made from carved wood, cloth, paint and bright ornaments. Traditionally, puppeteers use them to tell stories about local heroes, legends and everyday life, bringing music and movement together in a lively folk performance.

✦🏮✦

Light & decor

Handmade Paper Lamps

Handmade paper lamps and lanterns add a warm, festive glow to a room. Artisans cut, fold, layer and decorate paper with patterns inspired by traditional motifs. When used with a safe LED light, they create a beautiful ambience for celebrations or quiet evenings at home.

Textile traditions

Bandhani & Embroidery

Bandhani textiles are created by tying tiny sections of fabric before dyeing to form dotted patterns. Mirror work and colourful embroidery add texture and sparkle to bags, garments and home accessories, reflecting Rajasthan’s lively style.

Why handmade matters

Choosing handmade products supports skilled makers, keeps traditional knowledge alive and brings a little piece of Rajasthan’s culture into your home. Because each piece is crafted individually, small variations make every item unique.
